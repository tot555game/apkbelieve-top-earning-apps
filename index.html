What Are DTF Transfers?

DTF stands for Direct-to-Film. It is a modern printing method where a design is printed onto a special film. This film is then transferred onto fabric using a heat press. Unlike older methods, DTF works on almost any type of material, including cotton, polyester, and blends. It also works on dark and light colors because the process includes a white base layer in the design.

For a long time, creating custom shirts required expensive equipment or big minimum orders. Custom DTF Transfers Jacksonville have changed this. Now, you can order just one transfer or a thousand, depending on your need. This makes it perfect for beginners and professionals alike.

How to Use DTF Transfers (Easy Steps)

If you are new to this, do not worry. The process is much simpler than screen printing. Anyone can do this with a heat press. Here is a simple breakdown:

  1. Design Your Artwork: Create your image on a computer.
  2. Upload to a Printer: Send your file to a provider offering Custom DTF Transfers Jacksonville.
  3. Receive the Transfer: They will print the design on a special film and mail or deliver it to you.
  4. Apply the Heat: Place the film on your shirt and use a heat press for about 10‑15 seconds at 300°F.
  5. Peel: Let it cool and peel the film off. The design stays on the shirt.

Important Tip for Washing: To make the design last, turn your shirt inside out before washing. Use cold water and air dry or use a low heat setting. Avoid bleach. This keeps the colors bright and prevents cracking.
